Sources & Third Parties

LIRA uses a few external projects and services for Discovery content and optional on-device transcription. Your imported personal library stays on device.

  • WhisperKit On-device speech-to-text used for optional audiobook transcripts. Model files may be downloaded when you start transcription.
  • Hugging Face model hosting Used by the transcription stack to fetch the speech model the first time transcription is enabled.
  • LibriVox Public-domain audiobook catalog used in Discovery.
  • Open Library Book metadata and public-domain ebook discovery.
  • Internet Archive Media resolution and downloads for selected public-domain titles.
  • Apple frameworks Playback, speech synthesis, lock-screen controls, and system pickers are provided by iOS.

Those projects are owned by their respective organizations and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.
