Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

LIRA is built to keep your listening library on your device. This policy explains what information is handled, when the app uses the network, and how you can remove data.

Who we are

LIRA is published by Zeffou / Tumba Mulamba. For privacy questions, contact tumba.miller@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account is required to use the core app.
  • Imported books, bookmarks, progress, and optional profile details are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Network is used when you browse Discovery content or when you choose to download an on-device speech transcription model.

Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use LIRA, the app may store:

  • Library media — audiobooks and EPUB files you import, plus cover art extracted from those files.
  • Listening state — resume position, playback preferences, bookmarks, and sleep-timer settings.
  • Optional profile — display name, email, and profile photo if you add them in Settings. These stay on the device and are not required to use the app.
  • Transcripts — text generated on device when you start transcription for a book, plus any transcript files you import.
  • Model cache — if you start transcription, LIRA may download a speech-to-text model for on-device use and keep it in app storage.

Network use

LIRA may connect to the internet to:

  • Load Discovery catalogs and cover/sample media from public sources such as LibriVox, Open Library, and the Internet Archive.
  • Download a Whisper speech model (via WhisperKit / Hugging Face infrastructure) the first time you start transcription.

Imported personal library files are not uploaded to our servers. LIRA does not require a LIRA cloud account.

Permissions

  • Photos — only if you choose a profile photo through the system photo picker.
  • Notifications — only if you enable sleep-timer warnings.
  • Background audio — so playback can continue with the screen locked.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

LIRA does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use App Tracking Transparency tracking. We do not run a separate analytics product inside the app today.

Children

LIRA is a general-audience reading and listening app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because core data lives on your device, you control deletion:

  • Delete individual books from your library to remove their media and related local state.
  • Edit or clear profile name, email, and photo in Settings.
  • Clear generated transcripts from the player when available.
  • Uninstall LIRA to remove remaining app data from the device.

If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to respond and handle your request.

Third parties

When Discovery or model download features are used, third-party services process network requests under their own terms and privacy practices. See Sources & Third Parties.

Changes

We may update this policy as LIRA evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Email tumba.miller@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests.
