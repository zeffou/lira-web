Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

LIRA is built to keep your listening library on your device. This policy explains what information is handled, when the app uses the network, and how you can remove data.

Who we are

LIRA is published by Zeffou / Tumba Mulamba. For privacy questions, contact tumba.miller@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account is required.
  • Imported books, bookmarks, progress, covers, and preferences are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Network is used for Discovery catalogs and, if you choose, downloading an on-device speech transcription model.

Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use LIRA, the app may store:

  • Library media — audiobooks and EPUB files you import or download from Discovery, plus cover art (extracted or chosen by you).
  • Listening state — resume position, playback preferences, bookmarks, sleep-timer settings, and appearance choices (such as dark mode or app icon).
  • Optional Lock Screen identity — if you choose Custom Now Playing details, a custom title, artist name, and optional cover image stored only on device.
  • Transcripts — text generated on device when you start transcription for a book.
  • Model cache — if you start transcription, LIRA may download a speech-to-text model for on-device use (often around 500 MB the first time).

Network use

LIRA may connect to the internet to load Discovery catalogs from LibriVox, Project Gutenberg (via Gutendex), Open Library, and the Internet Archive, and to download a Whisper speech model the first time you start transcription. Imported personal library files are not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

  • Photos — only if you pick a custom book cover or a custom Lock Screen Now Playing image through the system photo picker.
  • Notifications — only if you use a sleep timer that schedules a warning notification.
  • Background audio — so playback can continue with the screen locked.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

LIRA does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use App Tracking Transparency tracking.

Children

LIRA is a general-audience reading and listening app. It is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete individual books from your library.
  • Clear custom Lock Screen Now Playing details and cover in Settings.
  • Clear generated transcripts from the player when available.
  • Uninstall LIRA to remove remaining app data from the device.
