Legal / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

LIRA keeps your listening library on your device. An account is optional. This policy explains what stays local, what Sign in with Apple and Supabase store if you create a profile, and how Lira Pro purchases are handled by Apple.

Who we are

LIRA is published by Zeffou / Tumba Mulamba. For privacy questions, contact tumba.miller@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account is required to import books, listen, or read.
  • Imported books, bookmarks, progress, covers, transcripts, and preferences stay on your device.
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple, we store a profile (display name and avatar) in Supabase.
  • Lira Pro is billed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use third-party advertising or App Tracking Transparency tracking.

Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use LIRA, the app may store:

  • Library media — audiobooks and EPUB files you import or download from Discovery, plus cover art.
  • Listening state — resume position, playback preferences, bookmarks, sleep-timer settings, and appearance choices (such as the bright or dark theme, or app icon).
  • Optional Lock Screen identity — if you choose Custom Now Playing details, a custom title, artist name, and optional cover image stored only on device.
  • Transcripts — text generated on device when you start transcription for a book.
  • Model cache — if you start transcription, LIRA may download a speech-to-text model for on-device use (often around 500 MB the first time).

Optional Sign in with Apple and profile storage

You can use LIRA without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates you and may share with us:

  • A unique Apple user identifier
  • Your name, if you choose to share it
  • An email address, or an Apple Hide My Email relay address

We use that information to create an optional LIRA profile. Profile data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage), which acts as our processor:

  • Display name — shown in the app if you set one.
  • Avatar image — if you pick a photo, it is uploaded to Supabase Storage and linked to your profile.
  • Account identifiers — needed to sign you back in and to delete the account when you ask.

Your imported library, listening progress, bookmarks, and transcripts are not uploaded to Supabase as part of this profile. Sign in with Apple is used so you can restore the profile, not to host your books.

You can use Hide My Email in Apple’s sign-in sheet. You can change your display name or avatar in LIRA Settings. To remove the cloud profile, use Delete account in Settings, or email us from the address associated with the account.

Lira Pro purchases

If you subscribe to Lira Pro, payment is processed by Apple. Apple may share a subscription status, original transaction identifier, and similar App Store receipt data with the app so we can unlock Pro features. We do not store your Apple ID password or full payment card number. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use for auto-renewal details.

Network use

LIRA may connect to the internet to:

  • Load Discovery catalogs from LibriVox, Project Gutenberg (via Gutendex), Open Library, and the Internet Archive.
  • Download a Whisper speech model the first time you start transcription.
  • If you sign in, contact Apple (Sign in with Apple) and Supabase (profile and avatar).
  • If you subscribe, contact Apple to validate Lira Pro.

Imported personal library files are not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

  • Photos — only if you pick a custom book cover, a Lock Screen Now Playing image, or a profile avatar through the system photo picker.
  • Notifications — only if you use a sleep timer that schedules a warning notification.
  • Background audio — so playback can continue with the screen locked.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

LIRA does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use App Tracking Transparency tracking.

Children

LIRA is a general-audience reading and listening app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children via an account.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete individual books from your library.
  • Clear custom Lock Screen Now Playing details and cover in Settings.
  • Clear generated transcripts from the player when available.
  • If you signed in: Settings → Account → Delete account removes the Supabase profile, avatar, and Sign in with Apple association we store. You can also email tumba.miller@gmail.com. We process requests within a few business days.
  • Uninstall LIRA to remove remaining app data from the device. Apple keeps subscription records according to Apple’s policies.

Third parties

Processors and catalogs we rely on are listed on Sources & Third Parties. Supabase may process profile data outside your country; we use them only to provide the optional account you requested.
