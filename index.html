Audiobook & EPUB player

Your books, on your device.

Import the audiobooks and EPUBs you already own. Resume where you left off, follow along with on-device transcripts, and keep your library on your iPhone.

How it works

Three quiet steps.

From empty shelves to a listening session.

01

Import

Add your own files, or browse public-domain titles from LibriVox, Gutenberg, and Open Library.

02

Listen

Speed, skip, sleep timer, and lock-screen controls — with the cover front and center.

03

Follow along

Optional on-device transcripts so the words can sit under the voice while you listen.

Features

Built for books you own.

A calm player — listen, read, and keep everything local.

Library

Imported audiobooks, EPUBs, progress, bookmarks, and covers live on your device.

Playback

Sleep timer, speed, skip, and Now Playing on the Lock Screen — including a custom title if you want one.

Reader

Day and night themes, font controls, and text-to-speech when you want the page spoken aloud.

Transcripts

Generate read-along text on device. The first run may download a speech model over Wi‑Fi.

Optional account

Sign in with Apple.

Create an optional LIRA profile with Sign in with Apple. Your display name and avatar are stored in Supabase so they can come back if you reinstall or switch devices. Your books, progress, and transcripts stay on the iPhone.

You can hide your email with Apple, change your avatar in Settings, or delete the account at any time. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and how to remove it.
