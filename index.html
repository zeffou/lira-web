Lira Pro

Monthly or yearly, billed by Apple

Lira Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Payment, renewal, and cancellation all go through your Apple ID.

Monthly

Lira Pro Monthly

Monthly / billed every month

Auto-renewable App Store subscription. The price for your country or region is shown in the app and on the App Store before you confirm.

  • Unlocks Lira Pro features listed in the purchase sheet
  • Renews each month until you cancel
  • Charged to the Apple ID used for purchase
Best value

Yearly

Lira Pro Yearly

Yearly / billed every year

Same Lira Pro access, billed once a year. The current yearly price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you confirm.

  • Unlocks Lira Pro features listed in the purchase sheet
  • Renews each year until you cancel
  • Charged to the Apple ID used for purchase

Auto-renewal. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.

Cancel via Apple. Manage or cancel anytime on your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → LIRA. You can also use Apple’s subscriptions page. Canceling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

Optional account

Sign in with Apple

Create an optional LIRA profile with Sign in with Apple. Your display name and avatar are stored in Supabase so they can come back if you reinstall or switch devices. Your books, progress, and transcripts stay on the iPhone.

You can hide your email with Apple, change your avatar in Settings, or delete the account at any time. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and how to remove it.
