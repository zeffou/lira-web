Common questions

Which formats can I import?

Audiobooks: MP3, M4A, M4B, AAC, WAV, CAF. Ebooks: EPUB. MOBI/AZW/PDF are not supported yet.

Where are my books stored?

On your iPhone/iPad, inside LIRA’s local library. There is no required LIRA cloud account.

Why does transcription download a large file?

Optional read-along transcription uses an on-device speech model. The first run may download about 500 MB over Wi‑Fi. After that, transcription can work without repeating the download.

How do I delete my data?

Delete books from your library, clear profile details in Settings, remove your photo, and/or uninstall the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a bug?

Email tumba.miller@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description. A screenshot helps.
