Optional account

Sign in with Apple is optional. It creates a LIRA profile (name and avatar) stored with Supabase. You may delete the account in Settings at any time. Deleting the account does not automatically cancel a Lira Pro subscription — cancel that separately through Apple.

Lira Pro subscriptions

Lira Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription purchased through the App Store. It unlocks the premium features described in the in-app purchase sheet. Core listening and reading of books you import does not require a subscription.

Plans

  • Lira Pro Monthly — auto-renewable, billed every one (1) month.
  • Lira Pro Yearly — auto-renewable, billed every one (1) year.

The price for your Apple ID country or region is shown in the App Store product page and in the purchase confirmation sheet before you pay. Prices may vary by location and are charged in the currency of your Apple ID.

Auto-renewal and Apple billing

  • Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the cost of the selected plan.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial, if one is offered, is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

How to cancel

Cancel through Apple, not through LIRA support email:

  • On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → LIRA → Cancel Subscription.
  • Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions and sign in with the same Apple ID.

Canceling stops the next renewal. You keep Lira Pro until the current paid period ends. Uninstalling LIRA does not cancel the subscription.
